Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

Orion Physics ("we", "our", "the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

Account information: Email address and password (or Apple ID) used to create your account.

Usage data: Quiz answers, scores, and progress statistics to provide personalised learning.

Exam preferences: Your selected exam level and exam board.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the App's features, including tracking your progress, recommending topics, and reviewing wrong answers.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers. We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to third parties.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings > Delete Account within the App. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Third-Party Services

We use the following services:

  • Supabase - Authentication and database
  • Apple StoreKit - Subscription management

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for students aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of any changes.
