Privacy Policy

OrionPhysics — Last updated 27 April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information OrionPhysics ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using OrionPhysics you agree to the practices described below.

What we collect

  • Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password so you can sign in across devices. We never see your password in plain text.
  • Study progress. Your answers to practice questions, scores, mock-test results, and review history are stored to your account so the app can show your progress and continue where you left off.
  • Purchase status. If you buy in-app purchases, we record which purchases your account has unlocked. Payment itself is processed entirely by Apple — we do not see or store credit-card details.

What we do not collect

  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or social-media trackers.
  • We do not collect your device contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers.

How your data is stored

Account and progress data are stored on Supabase, a third-party backend provider, and protected in transit by HTTPS/TLS. Apple processes all in-app purchase transactions per Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

OrionPhysics is intended for students aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and study data at any time by emailing tangeduer@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable period.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email tangeduer@gmail.com.
